## What you learned

* **The problem.** Frontier AI has collapsed the time from disclosure to exploit from weeks to hours, while enterprise patch cycles still run in weeks. AI-generated patches aren't the answer, and attackers increasingly target the software supply chain itself.
* **The offering.** TrueSource is a portfolio of commercially supported, verifiably built open source software: Spring Enterprise, TrueSource Trusted Artifacts, and TrueSource Data Services, sharing one standard of clean-room builds, CVE scanning, selective upstream patching, and early access.
* **The value.** Fixes from the upstream maintainers, available before disclosure and delivered as CVE-only patches, give back the time enterprises lose waiting, retesting, verifying, and chasing dependencies, and TrueSource can pay for itself by consolidating support and data services spend.
* **The use cases.** TrueSource covers every layer of a real application, from npm packages and Spring Boot services to data engines, and addresses scenarios from pre-disclosure CVEs to compliance mandates.
* **The hands-on experience.** You patched an application on an out-of-support Spring Boot line with a change to a single file, and verified the signature, provenance, and evidence of Bitnami Secure Images before pulling them.
