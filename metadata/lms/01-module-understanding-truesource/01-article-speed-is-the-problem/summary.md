AI has collapsed the time from disclosure to exploit from weeks to hours, while enterprise patch cycles still run in weeks. AI-generated patches aren't the answer: in independent testing, only 17 in 100 were robust fixes. Enterprises lose most of their time waiting for advisories, retesting upgrades, running versions that no longer get fixes, verifying builds, chasing transitive dependencies, and covering the rest of their stack, from other languages to container images and data engines.
