The capabilities behind TrueSource aren't new. Spring Enterprise, Application Advisor, Bitnami, and Broadcom's data services are already in production at large enterprises. Here's what some of them have achieved.

## Alight: removing roadblocks for application innovation

Alight delivers HR benefits to 35 million users. Its teams used Spring Application Advisor to upgrade their Spring applications continuously, instead of in large, disruptive projects.

* **70%** reduction in engineering time for maintenance upgrades
* **50%** cost reduction for running Spring microservices, with help from the Tanzu team to scale the environment for serverless components
* Upgrade cycles stretched from **every 6 months** to **every 1 to 2 years**

Alight reinvested the "found hours" into feature delivery and AI innovation, and refactored core legacy systems from a monolith into microservices.

> "Since we started using Application Advisor, we've seen a 70% reduction in engineering time tied to upgrades. Now we can focus more of our resources on new value-added features."
>
> — Chris Roberts, VP of Enterprise Architecture & IT Solutions, Alight

## A wealth management firm: a portfolio upgraded in 42 days

A wealth management firm in a highly regulated industry needed to secure a large open source Spring portfolio quickly. Upgrades of that size and scale had previously taken 12 to 17 months.

* The entire Spring portfolio, **3,300 repositories**, was scanned and upgraded by **one developer** using Application Advisor.
* More than **37,000** Spring containers were upgraded in **42 days**, instead of 17 months.
* More than **1,100 pull requests** were created **three days before the CVEs were published**, thanks to early patch access and secure Java dependencies for Spring.
* Patching moved from manual effort to a fully automated pipeline, running every hour, around the clock, built in under six weeks.

> "This thing, Spring Application Advisor, is not leaving the company… it makes our life so much easier."
>
> — Technical Architect

## A financial services organization: from Oracle to PostgreSQL

A financial services organization wanted a more flexible, cost-effective, cloud-ready standard for database services. It adopted Tanzu for Postgres as a complete solution for its database needs:

* Multiple deployment types: bare metal, virtual machines, and Kubernetes
* Enterprise customization for disaster recovery, high availability, auditing, and backup and restore
* A control plane for fleet management, delivering an on-premises DBaaS to its developers

Moving off expensive proprietary databases significantly reduced operational costs, while enterprise-grade features met its security and compliance requirements.

> "It's very easy to spin up Postgres databases in the cloud. But we wanted the flexibility to have a similar offering for Postgres as a service on premise. Postgres is our target database for all new workloads at the firm."
>
> — Director of Database Services

## A European bank: 35 TB from Oracle to PostgreSQL in six weeks

A European bank with more than 7,200 branch offices wanted to reduce operating expenses through license reduction, increase data locality, and drive automation across its data platform. It migrated about **35 TB** from Oracle to PostgreSQL on **VMware Data Services Manager**, on a vSphere vSAN stretched cluster for high availability, in **six weeks**. Application teams got a convenient, cloud-native DBaaS experience, and the IT team got a platform that's scalable and easy to manage without specialized know-how.
